ESMERINGHOY 27.0.0-devel is the requested package/in-app name, not a claim of independently authored or recompiled Mesa code. pt4droid supplies selectable Turnip rendering profiles before driver initialization; these profiles do not apply automatically in other apps. No profile is claimed faster without phone measurements. No GPU clocks, kernel, compression, LRZ or shader-precision changes are applied by the package.
The upstream Gen8 package targets A8xx; A7xx/Adreno750 use requires device comparison. Stock/system driver choices remain available. Full corresponding upstream patches and build recipe must be reconciled before a reproducible custom-source rebuild can be claimed.
